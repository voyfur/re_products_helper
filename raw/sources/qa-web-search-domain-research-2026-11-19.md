## Interpretation and boundaries

- The article/product card in Race Expert remains the product identity anchor. External pages must match brand/model and the relevant size, flavor, formulation, product form, and version; reject close-but-different products.
- For buyer experience, count at most one source per independent domain and require two exact-product domains before reporting a recurring subjective theme. Do not store or quote reviews, identities, or reviewer profile data.
- Manufacturer pages establish product-page facts, not independent buyer reviews. A search result or public listing is not evidence that automated reuse is permitted; review current platform terms before implementation.
- Research is category-representative, not exhaustive. No Tavily endpoint/API, OpenAI call, database, `.env`, test run, or deployment was used.
