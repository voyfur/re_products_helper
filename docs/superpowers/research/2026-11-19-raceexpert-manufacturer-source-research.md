# Race Expert manufacturer and Ukrainian distributor source research

**Research date:** 2026-11-19  
**Purpose:** identify credible first-party and explicitly authorized product-information domains that could support Web Search for products currently represented in Race Expert’s public catalog.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a representative snapshot of public category pages and visible brand filters, not a complete inventory of the store’s catalog. The category pages expose many brands and product variants, and the visible brand lists are not enough to prove every active SKU or an authorized relationship. [Race Expert’s energy-gel category](https://raceexpert.com.ua/sportivne-harchuvannya/geli-energetichni/), [protein category](https://raceexpert.com.ua/sportivne-harchuvannya/proteyinovi-dobavki/), [vitamins and supplements category](https://raceexpert.com.ua/vitamini-ta-minerali/), and [sports accessories category](https://raceexpert.com.ua/aksesuari/) are the catalog snapshots used here.

“Official product source” below means a brand or manufacturer-controlled product page/catalog. A local domain is called an **authorized Ukrainian distributor** only where there is direct evidence for that relationship; a distributor’s own claim is marked as self-claimed. A manufacturer page can confirm what a product variant is, but it does not replace the Race Expert article card as the identity anchor for a user’s question.

## Domains to hold out pending a specific product mapping

The store’s visible filters also show **IONTMAX, Fitly, Isostar, Bianchi, FIZI, Kevin Levrone, Lipobar, Miami Vibes, FitWin, Powerful, Bodyperson Labs, Crava, Fortuna, Go On, MST, Sporter, ESTEM, TRISLIDE, SHOWER PACK, Brave U, Weider, Olimp, Scitec, and Nature’s Way**. The category pages establish catalog visibility, but this pass did not verify a sufficiently clear manufacturer-controlled domain and exact product page for each name. Keep these out of the initial source allowlist until a product card and official source are paired. This is especially important for generic names (for example, Fitly) and similar-looking brand/company names.

## Recommendation for the Web Search source inventory

1. Use the Race Expert article/SKU and revalidated product card as the identity anchor; search only official brand pages and manufacturer-confirmed Ukrainian distributor domains mapped to that brand.
2. Store a per-brand mapping from Race Expert brand label to exact allowed hostnames. Keep distributor/retailer hosts separate from manufacturer hosts and record why each distributor is trusted. Avoid broad wildcard domains.
3. Require an exact match on product line/model plus flavor, format, quantity/size, and model version where those vary. A page for a similar product in the same brand should not qualify as confirmation.
4. Treat this list as a starting allowlist, not full coverage. Expand it only from actual Race Expert product records and after verifying each first-party page and any claimed Ukrainian distribution relationship.
